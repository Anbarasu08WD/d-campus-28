How the prediction works

From raw records to a clear next step.

The scoring is transparent. Every student can see which input moved their forecast. Read the full method →

  1. 01

    Record

    Administrators enter subject marks, mark class attendance and log assignment completion for each student.

  2. 02

    Predict

    A weighted model combines marks (60%), attendance (25%) and assignments (15%) into a performance score, an outcome band and a confidence level.

  3. 03

    Improve

    Weak subjects surface as focus areas. Students turn them into planner tasks with deadlines and watch the forecast move.
