How the prediction works

How D-campus predicts student performance.

The prediction is a transparent, rule-based score built from three things the college already records. There is no hidden step: every number below is the one the system uses.

Try the calculator ↓ See my forecast →
Step 1 · The three signals

What goes in.

Administrators record marks, attendance and assignments for each subject. The system combines them across all of a student’s subjects.

60%

Internal marks

Total marks scored divided by total marks available, across every subject. A 100-mark subject therefore counts more than a 20-mark one.

marks scored ÷ marks available × 100
25%

Attendance

Classes attended divided by classes held, across every subject. It is an early signal, because attendance usually changes before marks do.

classes attended ÷ classes held × 100
15%

Assignments

Assignments completed divided by assignments given, across every subject. It shows consistency of study habits.

assignments done ÷ assignments given × 100
Step 2 · The formula

How the score is calculated.

Each signal is multiplied by its weight and the three results are added. The weights are fixed and visible. They are a design choice of this project, not learned from data.

Performance score = 0.60 × Marks + 0.25 × Attendance + 0.15 × Assignments
Worked example
Marks 82%
82 × 0.60 = 49.20
Attendance 71%
71 × 0.25 = 17.75
Assignments 90%
90 × 0.15 = 13.50
Performance score
80.45 (shown as 80.5) → On track

Step 3 · The outcome

From a score to a band, a focus list and a confidence level.

85+

Advanced progress

Strong academic progress. Keep the habits that are working.

70 – 84.9

On track

Steady progress. Strengthen the subjects with the lowest marks.

50 – 69.9

Developing

Progress needs attention. Focus on attendance and assignments.

Below 50

Needs support

A focused improvement plan is recommended.

Focus areas

Any subject scoring below 60% is flagged as a focus area. Students can turn each one into a task with a due date in the study planner.

Confidence level

The estimate is marked Limited confidence when fewer than three subjects are recorded and Moderate from three subjects up. More records make the estimate more useful.

No records yet

If nothing has been recorded for a student, the system shows “Not enough data” instead of guessing.

What this prediction is, and what it isn’t.

  • It is an explainable estimate of current academic progress, computed from the records the college has entered.
  • It is not a guarantee of exam results or an official qualification, and it does not use information beyond marks, attendance and assignments.
  • It is only as good as the records. Out-of-date or missing entries lead to a less accurate estimate.
